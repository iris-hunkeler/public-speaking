  <tr>
    <th>Abstract</th>
    <td>Everybody talks about Serverless, but you don't know how to join the fun and get started? Don't worry, it's pretty simple! AWS Serverless allows you to quickly and cheaply build applications. This is especially useful for the rapid prototyping required during a hackathon or building an MVP to validate ideas - but also used for large production loads with varying load. In this session, we will look at what's behind this hype and for which use cases serverless is a good solution. We will also go through practical examples so you can join the Serverless party train!
